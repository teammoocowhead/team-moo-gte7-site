The short version

This app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We ain't that kind of person, fam, we just want you to enjoy the game.

Skill Tree Browser

Builds you plan are saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Steering Wheel + Gyro Aim

When you press START, the app sends your phone's motion-sensor readings and your screen touches directly to the IP address you typed in, which is meant to be your own PC running the game, over your local network. It is sent in real time and is not stored by the app. The game on your PC replies with status and vibration cues. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. The address you typed is remembered on your phone so you don't have to enter it again.

Permissions

  • Internet: only to reach your PC on your local network.
  • Vibrate: for vibration feedback.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions are used. The optional light burst uses Android's flashlight control only; the camera is never opened.

Links

The app's "From the Developer" page has buttons that open our Discord server and Steam page in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

The app keeps no data about you anywhere except on your own phone (your saved build and the PC address you typed). Delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. For any questions or requests, email the contact address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and in the app.
